# Quarterly Sales vs Quota: Management Summary (2013Q3)

## Headlines
1. Reseller sales were $7,644,680 for the quarter, 73.8% of quota.
2. That's up 31.5% versus the same quarter last year ($5,814,080 in 2012Q3).
3. Blended gross margin was just 0.3%, thin because Bikes, 86% of sales volume, ran at a -1.1% margin this quarter. Accessories (36.7%), Clothing (8.5%) and Components (7.1%) were all solidly profitable but too small a share of volume to offset Bikes.

## What changed
Sales grew year over year, but quota attainment was below 100% in every territory this quarter. Northeast (Michael Blythe, 87.8%) and Southwest (Linda Mitchell, 85.8%) were closest to target; Germany (Rachel Valdez, 61.8%) and Canada (Garrett Vargas, 59.8%) were furthest behind.

## What to watch
- Bike margin: it's driving the overall margin number, and it's currently negative.
- Germany and Canada attainment, if the pattern repeats next quarter.
